About

I am currently an MPhil student at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou).

I received my B.E. degree from Xinjiang University, School of Intelligence Science and Technology (School of Future Technology).

My research focuses on low-level vision, unified understanding-generation models, and representation learning.

I am open to research discussions and collaborations. Please feel free to contact me.
