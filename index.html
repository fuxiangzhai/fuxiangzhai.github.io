About

I am an MPhil student in the Artificial Intelligence Thrust, Information Hub, at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), advised by Prof. Lei Zhu.

My research focuses on image generation and generative multimodal models. I am particularly interested in making visual understanding actionable for generation through unified understanding-generation models, latent reasoning and control, and reward/evaluation models for generative systems.
